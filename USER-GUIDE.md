# User Guide

## 1. Pick your vehicle

Use the vehicle dropdown (top bar) or the Vehicles page. The active vehicle filters
procedures and flags eTorque/ESS-specific steps.

## 2. Run a repair procedure

Procedures → open a job → work the **Removal** tab top to bottom, ticking each step.
Progress is saved automatically per procedure.

- **Torque** buttons jump to the exact tightening spec.
- **3D** buttons open the viewer with that component highlighted and the assembly exploded.
- **Reset progress** clears your checkmarks when the next car rolls in.

## 3. Explore the 3D engine

- Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click any part to inspect it.
- The **Explode** slider separates the assembly; **Assemble** snaps it back.
- The info card shows service notes, torque, and every procedure step touching the part.

## 4. Diagnose a fault code

Diagnostics → type a code (e.g. `P0299`) → open the card for causes, pinpoint tests,
and one-click links to the relevant repair procedure.

## 5. Look up parts & torque

Parts and Torque pages are searchable tables. Clicking a part with a torque spec jumps
straight to that spec. Low-stock parts are flagged red/yellow.

## 6. Use VR (optional)

On a WebXR-capable browser with a connected headset, the 3D viewer shows **Enter VR**.
You can look around the engine at scale; full controller interaction is on the roadmap.
